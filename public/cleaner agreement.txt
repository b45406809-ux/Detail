1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 “Customer” means a person or entity requesting or purchasing cleaning services through Detail.

1.2 “Cleaner” means the individual or service provider registered with Detail to provide services through the Detail platform.

1.3 “Platform” means the Detail application, website, systems, technology, communication channels and related services operated or made available by Detail.

1.4 “Booking” means a request for services made by a Customer through the Platform and accepted by a Cleaner.

1.5 “Transaction” means any payment or other commercial transaction arising from a Booking or from a Customer introduced to the Cleaner through Detail.

1.6 “Commission” means the percentage or fee payable to Detail in respect of a Transaction.

1.7 “Business Day” means a day on which relevant banking and payment institutions in Zimbabwe are ordinarily operating, excluding applicable public holidays.

1.8 “Account” means the Cleaner’s Detail account and associated profile, balance, records and access credentials.

2.1 This Agreement governs the Cleaner’s registration, use of the Detail Platform and provision of services to Customers through Detail.

2.2 Detail operates a technology platform that facilitates connections between Customers and Cleaners.

2.3 Detail does not guarantee that a Cleaner will receive any particular number of Bookings, customers, income or minimum earnings.

2.4 The Cleaner understands that use of the Platform is subject to this Agreement, applicable Detail policies, Platform rules and applicable Zimbabwean law.

10.9 The Cleaner must not attempt to avoid or reduce Commission through false reporting, manipulation, under-declaration, cash concealment or off-platform transactions.
